Groundedness and Faithfulness in RAG

Groundedness measures whether a generated response is supported by the retrieved documents. A response is grounded if all its factual claims can be traced to the provided context.

Faithfulness measures whether the generation accurately represents the source material without adding unsupported information.

Common groundedness failures:
1. The model adds plausible-sounding but unsupported claims
2. The model combines information from multiple sources incorrectly
3. The model ignores retrieved context and relies on parametric knowledge
4. The model hallucinates citations that don't match the source

Even with perfect retrieval, generation can produce ungrounded responses. This is a fundamental limitation of RAG: retrieval and generation are separate stages, and the generator may not faithfully use the retrieved context.

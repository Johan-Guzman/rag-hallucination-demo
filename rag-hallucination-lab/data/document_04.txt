Citation Failure in RAG Systems

Citation failure occurs when a generated response includes a citation that does not accurately support the associated claim. This can manifest in several ways:

1. The cited document does not contain the claimed information
2. The cited document contains contradictory information
3. The citation is hallucinated (references a non-existent document)
4. The citation is partially correct but omits critical context

Citation accuracy requires both correct retrieval and faithful generation. A system may retrieve the right document but generate a claim that goes beyond what the document supports, then cite that document as evidence.

Evaluating citation quality requires checking the alignment between each claim and its cited source, not just verifying that retrieval succeeded.
